Architecture

How it works

The assets are raw customers, clean customers, the segments and the report, and the graph says what each of them needs. The runner materialises them in that order, writes the customers and the report as Parquet files and writes a line in the lineage log for every asset, with the run it belongs to and the number of rows. The customers are scored with the RFM model, by their fifth on the recency, the frequency and the money they are worth.
