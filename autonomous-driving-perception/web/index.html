The lane and the cars, found in the picture.

A classical perception baseline for road scenes: a threshold finds the lane, connected components find the vehicles, and the two are measured against the truth with the IoU of the lanes and of the boxes.

The capabilities

The lane of the road

The bright stripes of the scene are found with a threshold that is fitted and then measured on other scenes.

▣

The cars, not the stripes

A blob has to fill its own box, so the thin edge of a lane is never counted as a vehicle.

⇄

Matched by overlap

The IoU of two boxes decides whether a detection is the same object as the truth.

⚖

Measured on unseen scenes

The threshold is chosen on one part of the scenes and the metrics come from the other part.

⊘

No scene, no false alarm

An empty scene gives no detection at all instead of a box out of nothing.

⛁

Classical, and honest about it

Thresholding and connected components, not a trained network; the README says where the limit is.

Architecture

How it works

The lane is the part of the scene that is brighter than a threshold, and the threshold is chosen on one part of the scenes and measured on the rest, so the numbers are not the ones the threshold was picked for. The vehicles are the blobs of bright pixels that are not too small, not too thin and that fill their own box, which is how the thin edges of the lane are told apart from a car. The detections are matched with the truth by the IoU of the boxes, and every detection is used at most once.
