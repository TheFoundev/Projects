The words of a recording, one after the other.

Transcribe splits a recording into the parts where something is said, works out what every part is from its spectrum and hands back the sentence: the reference of the sample, the words that were heard and the error rate of the service.

Architecture

How it works

The service works on synthesized speech: every word of the vocabulary is a pair of tones with an envelope, and a phrase is the tones of its words with a pause in between. The waveform is cut into the parts where the energy is above the threshold, the spectrum of every part is turned into a log band vector, and a random forest reads the word out of that vector. The word error rate of the run is measured on the test phrases, where a word that is missing or wrong costs one error.
