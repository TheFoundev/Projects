The spike in the stream, before it is over.

Streamwatch reads a numeric signal point by point, keeps a running mean and variance of it and flags every point that stands too far from what came before, without ever looking at the whole series.

Architecture

How it works

The detector is an exponentially weighted moving average: the mean and the variance follow the signal, and the score of a point is how far it is from the mean in units of the standard deviation. The first points are skipped, so the detector has settled before it starts to judge. The threshold is not guessed: every candidate is tried on the stream and the one with the best F1 is kept, and the precision, the recall and the F1 are measured against the labels of the stream.
