Let the search try the space and keep the best.

A random search over the hyperparameters of a random forest: every trial is fitted, scored with the accuracy and the F1, and written to a SQLite trial log, and the service answers with the best parameters that have been found so far.

Architecture

How it works

The search space is three lists: the number of trees, the depth of the forest and the smallest number of samples in a leaf. Every trial draws a point from the space with a seeded generator, fits a random forest on the training split and scores it on the test split. The trial is appended to a SQLite table with its parameters, its accuracy and its F1, and the best accuracy seen so far is kept next to the parameters that produced it.
