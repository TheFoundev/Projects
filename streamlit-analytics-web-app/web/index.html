Architecture

How it works

The metrics are defined once, in src/pipeline.py, so the page and the API mean the same thing. The subscriptions are a table with a start month, a churn month and a monthly fee; the MRR of a month is the sum of the fee of the subscriptions that are running in it, the churn is the share of the subscriptions that ended in the month, and the net revenue retention compares what the old customers bring in with what they brought in before. The Streamlit page of the project filters the table and draws the numbers; this console reads the same functions and shows them as JSON.
