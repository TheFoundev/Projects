Recommendations that know the item, not only the user.

A recommender that mixes two scores: a truncated SVD over the ratings of the users, and a content score built from the tag profile of the items each user rated highly. The mix is measured with the precision at five of the held-out ratings.

The capabilities

Architecture

How it works

The ratings are turned into a user by item matrix, and a truncated SVD with eight components gives every user and every item a latent factor; the dot product of the factors is the collaborative score. The tags of the items a user rated four or higher are averaged into a profile, and its cosine with the tags of the catalogue gives the content score. The two are mixed with 0.7 and 0.3, and the items the user already rated are removed before the top five is taken.
