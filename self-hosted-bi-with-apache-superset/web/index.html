The questions you saved, answered on the data of today.

Mini BI keeps a small warehouse of orders in SQLite, runs the saved SQL queries of the project over it and puts the answers on one page: the revenue of every month, the products that pay, the share of every region and the share of the customers that come back.

Architecture

How it works

The pipeline builds a table of orders, writes it into artifacts/bi_warehouse.db and runs the four saved queries of the sql directory over it with pandas, so the answers come out of SQL and not out of Python. The result is written to artifacts/dashboard.json and the chart of the monthly revenue is drawn from the same rows. The Flask application of the project serves the dashboard and the JSON API of this site from that file.

The API

Endpoints

MethodPathWhat it does
GET/api/summaryThe queries, the orders, the revenue and the repeat rate of the warehouse
GET/api/queriesThe saved queries and the rows they answered with
GET/api/monthlyThe revenue of every month
POST/api/runRun the saved queries again and answer with the new dashboard
GET/chartThe monthly revenue of the last run
GET/demoThe dashboard of the project, unchanged
