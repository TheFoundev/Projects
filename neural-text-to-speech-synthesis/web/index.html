Text in, a waveform out, and a number that says how close it is.

Voxform is a formant synthesiser: the text is turned into phonemes by a letter-to-sound engine, the duration model says how long every phoneme lasts, the prosody gives the pitch, and the result is written as a WAV file that is measured instead of praised.

Architecture

How it works

The engine is explicit in every stage. The letter-to-sound rules turn the letters of a word into phonemes, with a lexicon for the words the rules get wrong. A duration model, a linear regression over the length of a word and its number of syllables, gives the time of every phoneme, and a prosody model gives the pitch, the stress and the rise of a question. Every vowel is rendered with its three formants, every consonant with noise, a burst or a nasal murmur, and the evaluation measures the formant peaks of the synthesized vowels with an FFT and compares them with the targets of the phonemes.

The API

Endpoints

MethodPathWhat it does
GET/api/summaryThe error of the duration model and the formant error of the corpus
POST/api/synthesizeSynthesize a phrase, write the WAV file and measure the formants
GET/api/phonemesThe phonemes of a phrase, word by word
POST/api/trainFit the duration model again and answer with the new error
GET/console.htmlThe console of this site
