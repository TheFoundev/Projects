A bicubic upscaler with a tunable unsharp mask: a low resolution image is scaled four times with bicubic resampling and then sharpened, and the result is measured against the sharp original with the PSNR and the mean absolute error.

Architecture

How it works

The dataset pairs a sharp image with a four times smaller bicubic downsample of it, so every low resolution image has its own truth. The upscaler resizes the small image back with bicubic interpolation and applies an unsharp mask whose radius and strength are the two parameters of the model. The training tries three strengths and keeps the one with the best PSNR on the generated images, and the evaluation reports the mean PSNR and the mean absolute error.

The API

Endpoints

MethodPathWhat it does
GET/api/summaryThe PSNR, the mean absolute error and the sharpening that was kept
GET/api/imagesThe score of every image of the validation set
POST/api/upscaleUpscale a fresh low resolution image and answer with the picture and its PSNR
