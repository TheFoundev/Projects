A retriever is only as good as the answers it grounds.

Groundscore measures a retrieval augmented pipeline: every question is answered with a passage that is looked up with TF-IDF, the answer is a sentence of that passage, and the hit rate, the token F1 and the faithfulness say how well the answer stays with the passage it came from.

The capabilities

≋

The token F1 compares the answer with the answer that was expected.

⊨

The share of the words of the answer that really stand in the passage it came from.

⧗

A report on disk

The evaluation is written to artifacts/evaluation.json and to a CSV file.

Architecture

How it works

The harness holds a small set of questions with their reference answer and a collection of passages about a campus. The passages are turned into a TF-IDF matrix, a question is matched against it and the passage that scores best is the one that is retrieved. The answer is the sentence of that passage with the most words in common with the question, and three measures are computed: the share of questions whose passage is the right one, the overlap of the answer with the reference answer, and the share of the words of the answer that really stand in the passage it was taken from.
