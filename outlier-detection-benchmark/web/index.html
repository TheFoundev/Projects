Four detectors, three worlds, one AUC.

Oddity measures four outlier detectors on three synthetic worlds: a z-score, an isolation forest, a local outlier factor and a one class SVM are fitted on the normal points of every world, and the ROC AUC says how well each of them finds the rows that do not belong.

Architecture

How it works

Three worlds are generated: a cloud of normal points with outliers around it, a ring of normal points with outliers inside and outside, and three clusters with outliers spread over the whole plane. The detectors are fitted on the normal points alone, so they never see an outlier while they learn. Each detector gives every point a score, the scores of the world are turned into one ROC AUC, and the mean over the three worlds tells which detector is the one to keep.
