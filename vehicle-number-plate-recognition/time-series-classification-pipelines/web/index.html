Architecture

How it works

Every series becomes seven numbers: the mean, the standard deviation, the range, the roughness, the mean jump of the sign, the dominant frequency of the spectrum and the share of the spectrum that sits in that frequency. A random forest is fitted on those features and reads the shape of a series it has never seen. The score of the run is the accuracy on the held out series, and the confusion matrix says which shape the classifier confuses with which.
