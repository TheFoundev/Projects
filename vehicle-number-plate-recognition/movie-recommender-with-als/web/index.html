A recommender that learns the taste of every viewer.

Reelwise fits a biased matrix factorisation on a MovieLens-style rating matrix: every viewer and every film gets a vector of its own, the ratings are trained with stochastic gradient descent, and the result is a ranked list of films the viewer has not seen yet.

Architecture

How it works

The ratings are simulated from the genre preferences of the viewers, so the ground truth of the data is known and the catalogue has thirty films with a title and a genre. The factorisation keeps a bias for every viewer, a bias for every film and eight latent factors, and it is trained with stochastic gradient descent over the training split. A fifth of the ratings is held back: the RMSE of the predictions on that split is the error of the model, and a proposal counts as a hit when the holdout says the viewer liked the film.
