Architecture

How it works

The parser reads the years, the degree, the number of the projects and the skills out of the text of the resume. The text and those numbers go into a TF-IDF matrix that a logistic regression scores, and every candidate gets the probability of being shortlisted. The candidates and their scores are stored in SQLite, and the questions of the interview are drafted from the skills of the resume and from the words of the job it does not mention.
