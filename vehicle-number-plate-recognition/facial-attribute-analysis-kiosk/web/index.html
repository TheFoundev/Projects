A face on the screen, and what the model sees in it.

A kiosk that draws a synthetic face, reads the pixels of it and says whether the face wears glasses and whether it smiles. The classifier is a multi-output random forest and both attributes are measured on a split the model never saw.

Architecture

How it works

The generator draws a 32 by 32 face: an ellipse for the head, two eyes, a mouth that is either flat or curved, and a pair of rings when the face wears glasses. The pixels of every face are flattened into a vector of 1024 numbers and a multi-output random forest is fitted on three quarters of them. The kiosk page renders a new face, asks the model for its two attributes and shows the accuracy of each of them.
