Eight command words, heard without a network.

Quietword recognizes spoken commands without a network and without a neural network: a word becomes a short tone pair, the sound is cut into frames of log-band features, and the frame sequence is matched against the templates of the vocabulary with a dynamic time warping distance.

Architecture

How it works

Every word of the vocabulary has its own pair of tones and its own length, so the sound of a word is a short envelope over three sine waves. The signal is cut into sixteen frames and the spectrum of every frame is pooled into eight log bands, which gives the vector the matcher works with. The templates of the vocabulary are the shortest recording of every word, and the distance between two frame sequences is the cost of the cheapest warping path between them, so a word that is spoken a little faster still matches.
