Read the document, not every line of it.

An extractive summarizer: the sentences of a document are scored with TF-IDF against the centre of the document, and the best ones become the summary. The length of the summary is searched over the corpus, and the one that scores best is the one that is kept.

Architecture

How it works

The document is split into sentences, the sentences are turned into TF-IDF vectors and every vector is compared with the centroid of the document; a small position bonus prefers the lead paragraph. The training searches the lengths one to four sentences and keeps the one with the best mean ROUGE-L on the corpus of eight documents. The Flask application of the project serves this site and the API behind it.
