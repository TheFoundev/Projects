Where the limbs are, read off the picture.

Kinemap finds the people in a frame and estimates the seven joints of each of them: the bright pixels are grouped into a left and a right person, the joints are placed on the bounding box of every person, and the accuracy is the PCK of the joints against the truth of the scene.

Architecture

How it works

The scenes are synthetic: two stick figures are drawn on a noisy dark background and the coordinates of their joints are kept. The estimator thresholds the image, splits the bright pixels in two groups around the middle of the frame and reads the joints off the bounding box of every group, so a person keeps its place in the picture. The PCK counts a joint as a hit when it is within a fraction of the size of the frame, and the threshold that gives the best score is the one the estimator keeps.
