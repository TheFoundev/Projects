Every answer points at the passage it came from.

Answerbook answers a question out of a small collection of documents: the documents are turned into a TF-IDF matrix, the passage that matches the question best is retrieved, and the answer is the sentence of that passage which is closest to the question.

Architecture

How it works

The corpus is a handbook of twelve short passages about a campus, and every passage has an identifier that is the name of its topic. The passages and the question are embedded with a TF-IDF vectorizer and compared with the cosine similarity, and the two best passages are kept. The answer is picked among the sentences of those passages, the one that shares the most words with the question, and the identifier of the passage is reported with it, so every answer can be pointed at.
