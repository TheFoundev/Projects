A point on the picture, and the object it points at.

Promptable segments an object out of a picture from a prompt: a point on the object, a box around it or a mask, and the answer is the mask of that object, its bounding box and its overlap with the truth.

The capabilities

Every panel below is served by the real API of this project — nothing here is a mock-up.

A point is enough

Click a pixel on the object and the mask of the object comes back.

The IoU of the predicted mask and the true mask says how good the answer is.

The threshold and the growth of the mask are fitted on the training pictures, not fixed by hand.

Architecture

How it works

The model has the two parts of a promptable segmenter. The encoder turns the picture into an embedding that holds the probability of every pixel and the labels of the connected bright regions; the decoder takes the embedding and the prompt and answers with the mask of the region the prompt points at. The parameters of the decoder, the brightness threshold and the growth of the mask, are fitted with a coordinate descent on the training pictures, and the loss of the run is one minus the mean IoU.

The API

Endpoints

MethodPathWhat it does
GET/api/summaryThe metrics of the model, the threshold it learned and the IoU of every picture
GET/api/infoWhat the service is and which prompts it takes
GET/api/predictionsThe prompt point, the pixels and the IoU of every picture of the set
POST/api/trainFine tune the decoder again and answer with the new metrics
POST/segmentSegment a picture with a point, a box or a mask
