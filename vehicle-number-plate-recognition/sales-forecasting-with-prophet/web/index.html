Tomorrow's sales, with the shape of the week in it.

Prophetly fits an additive model on a daily sales series: a straight trend, the effect of every day of the week and the yearly harmonics, and it forecasts the days that follow with the error it made on the days it did not see.

Architecture

How it works

The series of the project is built with a trend, a weekly sine, a yearly sine, four holiday bumps and noise, so the ground truth of the data is known. The model is additive: a straight line is fitted on the training days with a least squares fit, the days of the week are read off the rest of the series and the yearly harmonics are fitted on what is left. The last fifteen percent of the series is held back, the model forecasts it and the MAE and the MAPE of that forecast are the numbers of the run.
