Read a sentence and find the names in it.

Tagsmith labels every token of a sentence as a person, an organisation, a place or nothing: a logistic regression reads the shape of the word, its prefix and its suffix and its neighbours, and the entities of the text are the runs of tokens that carry the same label.

Architecture

How it works

The sentences are generated from templates with a gazetteer of people, organisations and places, so the labels of the training set are known. Every token is turned into a small feature vector: the word itself, its shape, whether it starts with a capital, its first and its last two letters and the words around it. A logistic regression is fitted on those features, and the entities of a sentence are rebuilt from the labels with a span reader that understands the BIO form, so a continuation belongs to the entity before it.
