Five forecasters, one holdout, no winner by default.

Tidemark compares forecasting baselines on a seasonal daily series: a moving average, a linear trend, a seasonal naive, a seasonal trend and SARIMAX all forecast the same holdout, and the mean absolute error and the RMSE of every one of them stand next to each other.

Architecture

How it works

The series is simulated with a trend, a weekly pattern, a monthly pattern and noise, and it is split into a training part and a holdout of the last fifth. The season of the series is not assumed but worked out from its own shape, so the week of a daily series is found and not hard coded. Every baseline forecasts the holdout and the errors are measured with the mean absolute error and the root mean squared error, and the same baselines can be measured again over several rolling windows instead of one single split.
