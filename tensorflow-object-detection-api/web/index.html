Where the objects are, and which one it is.

Detector finds the objects in a picture: the bright regions become the proposed boxes and a classifier reads the crop of every box and says whether it is a circle or a square.

The capabilities

The boxes of the bright things

The connected bright regions of the picture become the proposals of the detector.

A class for every box

The crop of the box is resized and read by a random forest, so every box has a class.

✓

The hits are counted

A proposal that overlaps a true box by 0.4 or more is a hit, and the rest are the misses.

◑

The precision of the boxes

The console shows the precision and the recall of the proposals, not only the accuracy of the class.

⛨

The small ones are dropped

A bright region that is too small is not proposed, so the detector does not guess on noise.

The console trains the classifier again and shows the metrics of the new run.

Architecture

How it works

The detector has the two parts of a real detector. The proposals come from the connected bright regions of the picture, and the smallest ones are thrown away, so a box is only proposed when there is enough of it in the picture. The crop of every box is resized to a fixed size and becomes the features of a random forest, which reads the class of the box. The precision and the recall of the boxes are measured against the truth of the scenes, with a proposal that overlaps a true box by at least 0.4 counted as a hit.
