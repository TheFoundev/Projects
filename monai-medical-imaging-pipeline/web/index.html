A lesion in a volume, found by a threshold and checked with Dice.

A small 3D pipeline: synthetic volumes hold a bright spherical lesion, a percentile threshold segments it, the segmentation is scored with the Dice coefficient, and a logistic regression on three features of the volume classifies the patient.

Architecture

How it works

Every volume is a cube of noise around 0.30 in which a sphere of radius two to four is raised to about 0.78, and half of the volumes carry one. The segmentation keeps the voxels above a percentile of the volume, and the percentile is searched over four values and kept by the mean Dice of the training volumes. Three features of the segmented volume feed a logistic regression that says whether the patient carries a lesion, and both scores are measured on the held-out volumes.

The API

Endpoints
