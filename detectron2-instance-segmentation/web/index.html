Architecture

How it works

The image is thresholded, the connected components of the bright pixels are labelled, and every component that is big enough becomes one instance with a mask of its own. The masks are compared with the ground truth by the IoU, and the best match of every truth is used, so a mask is never counted twice. The training sweeps the thresholds and keeps the one with the best mean IoU.
